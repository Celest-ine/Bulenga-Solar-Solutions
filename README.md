# Bulenga Solar Solutions Website

A responsive business website developed for **Bulenga Solar Solutions**, a solar energy solutions company based in Kenya.

The website is designed to provide customers with information about the company, its solar products and services, and ways to request quotations or get in touch with the business.

> **Project Status:** Client Review — Not Officially Deployed
> The website is currently under review and approval by the client. The GitHub Pages deployment is being used temporarily as a development/demo environment and should not be considered the company's official website.

## Overview

The project provides a modern, responsive web presence for Bulenga Solar Solutions with a focus on:

* Company information
* Solar product catalogue
* Solar installation and related services
* Product details
* Quote/request forms
* Contact information
* Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices

## Features

### Company Information

* Home page introducing Bulenga Solar Solutions
* About page describing the company
* Services section
* Contact information and business details

### Product Catalogue

* Structured solar product catalogue
* Product categories
* Individual product detail pages
* Fox ESS product information
* Product specifications without displaying product prices

### Customer Enquiries

* Quote request form
* Contact options
* WhatsApp contact integration
* Email contact functionality

### Responsive Design

The website is designed to work across:

* Desktop
* Tablet
* Mobile devices

## Technology
