Our Solar Services

Bulenga Solar Solutions is more than a product supplier — we support customers through the full process of moving to solar energy.

Solar System Installation

Installation of solar panels, inverters and related equipment for residential and business properties, carried out by our team from initial setup through to commissioning.

Solar Energy Consultation

Guidance on which type of solar and storage solution suits your property, energy usage patterns and goals — helping you make an informed decision before committing to a system.

Solar System Design

Planning how a solar and storage system fits your specific property, including product selection appropriate to your available roof space, phase supply and energy needs.

Energy Storage Solutions

Battery storage systems that let you store solar energy generated during the day for use in the evening or during grid outages.

Solar System Upgrades

Adding battery storage or expanding capacity on an existing solar installation, where compatible with your current equipment.

Maintenance & Support

Ongoing support for installed systems, so you have someone to contact if you have questions or notice an issue with your solar equipment.
