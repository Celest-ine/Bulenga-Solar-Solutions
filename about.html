About Bulenga Solar Solutions

Solar products and installation services for homes and businesses across Kenya.

Who We Are

Property. Power. Possibility.

Bulenga Solar Solutions is a solar solutions company serving customers in Kenya. We provide solar products and solar installation and energy solutions, using Fox ESS inverters, batteries, EV chargers and monitoring equipment as the technology behind our systems.

Our purpose is straightforward: help homeowners and businesses in Kenya move toward reliable, self-generated solar power. That means guiding customers through the options available, supplying suitable equipment, and installing systems designed around each property's needs — rather than offering a single one-size-fits-all package.

As a company, we work directly with customers from the first enquiry through to installation and ongoing support, so that solar energy feels approachable rather than overwhelming.

Our Relationship With Fox ESS

Fox ESS Products, Supplied by Bulenga

Fox ESS (FoxESS Co., Ltd.) is a manufacturer specialising in the research, production and sale of solar inverters and energy storage battery systems. Bulenga Solar Solutions offers a range of Fox ESS solar and energy storage products to customers in Kenya as part of our solution — Fox ESS is the equipment manufacturer, and Bulenga Solar Solutions is the company you work with for advice, supply and installation.
